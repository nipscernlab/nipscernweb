Application of Wiener Filter and Sparsity Theory for Pulse Unfolding from Scintillator Detector

Tiago Motta Quirino, Guilherme Cit, Gabriel Cesário, Sérgio Inácio, Antônio Pereira, André Massafferri, Luciano Manhães de Andrade Filho, Diogo Rocha, Jorge Amaral

8th International Symposium on Instrumentation Systems, Circuits and Transducers

Open PDF Publications

Abstract

This study introduces an unfolding algorithm that integrates the Wiener Filter and sparsity theory to effectively narrow the width of pulses in time, addressing the challenge of pile-up effects in environments with substantial electronic noise. The algorithm is tested with data from a cosmic ray setup experiment composed of a scintillator and a silicon photomultiplier (SiPM). The algorithm’s performance highlights its potential in improving the consistency of peak positioning and pulse resolution, which are crucial for precise radiation measurement.
