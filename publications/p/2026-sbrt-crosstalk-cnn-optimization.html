Optimization strategies for CNN's aiming crosstalk mitigation in finely segmented calorimetry systems

André Paiva Conrado Rodrigues, Antonio Carlos Lopes Fernandes Junior, Paulo Cesar Machado de Abreu Farias, Eduardo Furtado de Simas Filho, Edmar Egidio Purcino de Souza, Mateus Hufnagel Maranha de Faria, Luciano Manhães de Andrade Filho, Bertrand Laforge

Simpósio Brasileiro de Telecomunicações e Processamento de Sinais — SBrT 2026

Open PDF DOI 10.14209/sbrt.2026.1571299609 Official page Publications

Abstract

Calorimeters with fine sensor segmentation used in High Energy Physics experiments suffer from mutual interference due to electromagnetic coupling between neighboring sensor cells. Crosstalk affects the characterization and measurement of incident particle properties, like energy and time of flight, degrading the quality of event reconstruction. This work proposes different optimization strategies for a convolutional neural network using simulation-generated events, aiming to mitigate crosstalk effects. Using a strategy with per cell normalization, it was possible to enhance the timing estimation for the central cell, with a mean R² of 0.893, in comparison with 0.721 from the baseline.
