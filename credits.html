Data

HYG Stellar Database CC BY-SA 4.0 5,044 stars
Hipparcos, Yale and Gliese combined. The sky over the home page is this catalogue drawn as it is: every star down to magnitude 6 at its own position, with its own brightness and its own colour. Taken in the packed form d3-celestial distributes.
astronexus.com/projects/hyg
TileCal reference pulse shape ATLAS Collaboration
The figure on the CERN page. The shaped pulse, its 150 ns width, the seven samples 25 ns apart and the out-of-time signal from the following bunch crossing all come from J. M. de Seixas, on behalf of the ATLAS Collaboration, "The TileCal Energy Reconstruction for LHC Run2 and Future Perspectives". The curve is redrawn from the published landmarks and tabulated by tools/build-tilecal-pulse.js; the sample values follow from it and are not measured ADC counts.
arXiv:1510.01690

NIPS-CERN Projects

The following research software developed by NIPS-CERN is featured on this website and is available as open source.

SAPHO Processor Architecture Framework
Simple Architecture for Processor Hardware and Organisation — FPGA processor design platform used for teaching digital logic at UFJF.
View project
YANC Compiler Toolchain
Yet Another NIPS-CERN Compiler — CMM-to-assembly and assembly-to-machine-code compiler for the SAPHO architecture.
View project
HITS FPGA Simulator
Real-time FPGA simulator of the ATLAS Tile Calorimeter readout, running on a DE10-Nano board.
View project
AURORA IDE for SAPHO
The desktop IDE for SAPHO: write C±, then compile, simulate and inspect in one window, with the Aurora Intelligence assistant.
View project
CGV ATLAS Calorimeter Viewer
Interactive 3D visualisation of the ATLAS calorimeter system (TileCal, LAr barrel, HEC endcaps) built with Three.js and Vite.
View project
