Our Mission

To bring engineering, software, particle physics and logic together into concrete tools, architectures and systems that scientific research can actually use, and to prepare the next generation of scientists by putting undergraduates inside real experiments alongside CERN researchers.

What defines NIPS-CERN is where its undergraduates work. They are inside real CERN experiments, on active physics analyses and detector instrumentation, next to international researchers.

Formerly LAPTEL. The Brazilian laboratory has carried three names. It began as LAPTEL, the Signal Processing and Telecommunications Laboratory; became NIPS, the Centre for Instrumentation and Signal Processing; and took the name NIPS-CERN when the CERN collaboration was formalised and the group gained its second laboratory, at Route Salam in Meyrin. In Brazil the group works from the PPEE building, home of the Graduate Program in Electrical Engineering at UFJF.
