Who We Are

Science, Engineering, and Collaboration

NIPS-CERN unites undergraduate students, researchers, and world-class scientists in the pursuit of knowledge at the edge of modern physics.

Our Mission

To bring engineering, software, particle physics and logic together into concrete tools, architectures and systems that scientific research can actually use, and to prepare the next generation of scientists by putting undergraduates inside real experiments alongside CERN researchers.

What defines NIPS-CERN is where its undergraduates work. They are inside real CERN experiments, on active physics analyses and detector instrumentation, next to international researchers.

Formerly LAPTEL. The group was originally known as LAPTEL (Signal Processing and Telecommunications Laboratory) before being renamed NIPS (Centre for Instrumentation and Signal Processing) and later NIPS-CERN following the formalisation of the CERN collaboration. Our laboratory is located in the PPEE, the Graduate Program in Electrical Engineering, building at UFJF.
