SAPHO

Scalable-Architecture Processor for Hardware Optimization

Most processors are built first and programmed later. SAPHO turns that around: you write the algorithm in C±, a small dialect of C, and the toolchain emits a Verilog processor shaped by that exact program. The die behind this panel is the idea itself: gold blocks were asked for by the code; dark ones were never built.
