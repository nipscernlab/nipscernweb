SAPHO

Scalable-Architecture Processor for Hardware Optimization

Most processors are built first and programmed later. SAPHO turns that around: you write the algorithm in C±, a small dialect of C, and the toolchain emits a Verilog processor shaped by that exact program. Word width, memory sizes, even which ALU blocks exist. Divide nowhere, carry no divider.
