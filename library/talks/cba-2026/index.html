Keys

  • →, space or Page Down: next slide; on a slide with a video, the first press plays it
  • ← or Page Up: previous slide
  • Click on the frame: plays the video; click on the video: pauses it
  • F: full screen
  • N: presenter notes
  • H: this list
  • #n at the end of the address opens slide n
  • On a touch screen, swipe sideways to move
